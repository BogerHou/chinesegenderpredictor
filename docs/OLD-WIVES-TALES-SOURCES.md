# Old wives’ tales guide: source review

Checked 2026-10-09 for `/old-wives-tales-gender-prediction/`. The page body is in `src/old-wives-tales.mjs`; the standard article wrapper supplies the real maintainer byline. This is a folklore/party-game guide, not medical advice or a symptom assessment. No claim of a clinical reviewer or endorsement is made.

| Topic | Source | Supported statement and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bump shape | [Perry DF, DiPietro J, Costigan K, Birth, 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10655817/) | Questionnaire study of 104 women found no systematic relation between abdominal shape and fetal sex. Do not generalize this small study to every measurement or attribute its findings to other folklore tests. |
| Heart rate | [Bracero LA et al., J Matern Fetal Neonatal Med, 2016; online 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25754210/) | 655 pregnancies, 8–13 weeks; no significant male/female heart-rate difference. The wording is limited to first-trimester rate guesses, not a claim that all cardiac measures are identical throughout pregnancy. |
| Morning sickness | [Young NR et al., Arch Gynecol Obstet, 2021; online 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33098451/) | Retrospective self-report covering 4,320 pregnancies found a group-average difference in nausea frequency. The study did not validate a home diagnostic rule for an individual. Preserve the association rather than claiming that nausea and fetal sex have never been related. |
| Skin and hair | [Cleveland Clinic, Pregnancy glow, 2023-12-06](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/pregnancy-glow) | Official hospital explanation of hormone/blood-flow effects on skin and hormone-related hair changes. Its clinician does not endorse appearance as a sex-prediction method. Not a source for an individual diagnosis. |
| Food cravings | [NHS week 5 pregnancy guide](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/pregnancy/week-by-week-guide-to-pregnancy/1st-trimester/week-5/) | Describes cravings in relation to hormone-related taste/smell changes. Does not supply a validated sweet/salty-to-sex rule. No year is invented because the retrieved article has no visible review date. |
| Ring swing | [Cantergi D, Awasthi B, Friedman J, Hum Mov Sci, 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34607165/) | Motion capture demonstrated how finger movements drive hand-held pendulum oscillations. This is not a pregnancy or fetal-sex study. It explains the movement, not the success rate of any ring myth. |
| Chinese calendar | [Villamor E et al., Paediatr Perinat Epidemiol, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20618730/) | 2,840,755 singleton births; no agreement beyond chance for the studied calendar method. The guide links the site's accuracy page for detail and its additional 2023 source. Not evidence about cravings, rings or other folklore. |
| Prenatal information | [NHS ultrasound guidance, reviewed 2023-11-13](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/your-pregnancy-care/ultrasound-scans/) | Fetal sex may be visible at a mid-pregnancy scan depending on hospital policy and fetal position; cannot be 100% certain. No procedure is recommended solely for an announcement. |
